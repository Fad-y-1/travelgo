FAQ

Can I book for more than one traveler?

Yes. Open any trip, choose the number of travelers, select the date, then press Book Now.

Are sea trips available in Hurghada and Sharm El Sheikh?

Yes. TravelGo currently offers sea trips in both Hurghada and Sharm El Sheikh.

Booking Guide

  1. Choose Hurghada or Sharm El Sheikh.
  2. Open the trip you like.
  3. Select travelers and trip date.
  4. Press Book Now and complete the payment form.

Cancellation Policy

Guests can contact TravelGo support to request a cancellation or date change. Final approval depends on the trip date, availability, and supplier policy.
